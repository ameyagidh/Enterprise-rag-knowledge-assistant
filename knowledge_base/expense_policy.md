# Employee Expense Policy

## Reimbursable expenses

- Travel: flights (economy class for trips under 6 hours, premium economy allowed above that), trains, and rideshare to/from airports or client sites.
- Lodging: hotel or equivalent lodging up to $250/night in major metro areas, $180/night elsewhere.
- Meals: up to $75/day while traveling for business, itemized receipts required for any single expense over $25.
- Software and tools: any tool under $50/month can be expensed directly without prior approval; anything above that requires manager sign-off before purchase.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

- Alcohol (except at approved client dinners, up to $30/person).
- Personal entertainment, movies, or in-flight Wi-Fi for personal use.
- First-class flights or luxury hotel upgrades.

## Submission process

Submit all expense reports within 30 days of the expense through the Expensify portal. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed except with VP-level approval. Attach itemized receipts for any expense over $25.

## Approval chain

Expenses under $500 are auto-approved after manager review. Expenses over $500 require both manager and finance approval before reimbursement is processed.
